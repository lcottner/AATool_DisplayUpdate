The main objective of this repository is to add a pop-up to the display which shows progress for certain advancments. This will make it easier for the viewers to keep track of how far along the runner is in the speedrun.

This is mainly tracking for complex advancements. e.g. 2 by 2, A Complete Catalogue, A Balanced Diet, etc.

This pop up will only appear after the runner has started progress towards an advancement.
The pop ups will refresh when the tracker refreshes. They will be in a rotation until the advancement is completed.
This pop up will appear under the third row of the tracker.


Other things:
I want to tinker with the refresh rate of the tracker to see if the pop up display can sync to the action in game.
